## Program

The year 2027 opens with the 30th event of [PyData Venice]({{ site.baseurl }}/about-us/): our first one-day event, on 27 February 2027 at the Scientific Campus of Ca' Foscari University of Venice, in Mestre.
Two parallel tracks, one on AI 360° and one on Data Science, with talks given by practitioners and academics, and a round table to close the day.

Due tracce in parallelo, una su AI 360° e una su Data Science, con talk tenuti da tecnici e accademici, e una round table a chiudere la giornata.
