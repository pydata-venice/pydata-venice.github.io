## PyData

[PyData](https://pydata.org/) is an educational program of NumFOCUS, a non-profit foundation that [supports](https://numfocus.org/sponsored-projects) open source libraries such as matplotlib, pandas and scikit-learn, and has [affiliated](https://numfocus.org/sponsored-projects/affiliated-projects) libraries such as polars, cpython and marimo.

PyData promotes the discussion of best practices, new approaches and emerging technologies for data management, processing, analytics and visualization. PyData communities approach data science using many languages, including (but not limited to) Python, Julia and R.

There are [106 groups](https://www.meetup.com/pro/pydata) in 43 countries, with more than 180 thousand members in total.
In Italy there are officially 3 active groups: in Milan, Rome and Venice.

## PyData Venice

The Venice group is called [PyData Venice](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-venice/): it has more than 1456 members and 28 events.

After attending yet another PyCon Italia, Fabio Lamanna and Omar Rampado decided to start this adventure: PyData Venice was born as PyData Veneto in September 2019.
In the summer of 2023 Alessandra arrived, found the group paused for a year, and asked to carry it on.

## How we organize our meetups

Alessandra introduced two talks per meetup, and streaming even when the meetup is in person.

A key point of the group is to keep an academic audience, besides industry practitioners: everything lies in keeping the technical level high. Two talks per meetup make this possible: speakers can have different backgrounds, with at least one academic or conceptually advanced talk.

Each meetup has **short high-level talks**, where speakers share goals, challenges, failures and achievements, and a **discussion after each talk** on the adopted solution, the choices, doubts and questions. Talks are given **only by practitioners explaining to other practitioners** what they have hands-on experience with, no fluff. Meetups are held **in person** and always also in **streaming**, then saved on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@pydatavenice) for those who come later.

## Other initiatives

Besides the meetups, we organize thematic workshops, both locally and at conferences together with [PyCon Italia](https://pycon.it/).

The year 2027 opens with the 30th event of the group: [PyData Venice Day]({{ site.baseurl }}/pydata-venice-day/), the first one-day event.

## Come organizziamo i meetup

Alessandra ha introdotto due speech per meetup, e lo streaming anche quando il meetup è in presenza.

Un punto chiave del gruppo è mantenere un pubblico accademico, oltre che tecnici del settore: tutto sta nel mantenere alto il livello tecnico. I due speech per meetup permettono di raggiungere questo obiettivo: possono esserci speaker di livello differente, con almeno uno accademico o concettualmente avanzato.

Ogni meetup prevede **speech corti ad alto livello**, in cui gli speaker condividono obiettivi, sfide, sconfitte e traguardi, e una **discussione dopo ogni speech** sulla soluzione adottata, sulle scelte, sui dubbi e sulle domande. Gli speech sono tenuti da **soli tecnici che spiegano ad altri tecnici** ciò che hanno toccato con mano, nofuffa. I meetup si svolgono in **presenza** e sempre anche in **streaming**, poi vengono salvati su [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@pydatavenice) per i postumi.
